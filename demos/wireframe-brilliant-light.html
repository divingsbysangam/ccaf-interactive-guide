Workflow or agent? The first fork

~2 min

The first architectural question is never "which model" — it's how much autonomy the problem needs. If the steps are known in advance, build a workflow: fixed steps with LLM calls inside. If the model must decide its own path, that's an agent…

From zero — plain language & a familiar-platform analogy

Salesforce lens: a workflow is a Flow — every branch drawn in advance. An agent is like handing a case to a senior rep…

Q1

Agentic Architecture

A lending company processes thousands of applications daily. Every application goes through the same three stages: pull key fields from uploaded documents, check them against eligibility rules, and produce a draft decision letter. The team wants to add Claude to speed this up. What should they build?

A. Tempting because "agent" sounds like the modern answer — but autonomy buys nothing here and costs predictability. The steps never vary.

B ✓. When the steps are known in advance and repeated at volume, a deterministic workflow with gates is cheaper, faster, auditable, and easier to debug.
