Orbs, the way you meant.

A sphere drawn as a lattice of dots — the same language as the logo mark, made into a living element. Adapted in the spirit of Jakub Antalik's thinking-orbs, redrawn for paper and our tokens.

1 · Three states

Dense dotted globe on latitude rings, so meridians read as structure (closer to Antalik's). Grey = ahead (still). Blue = you are here (rotating, slowly). Gold = done (still, densest). Under prefers-reduced-motion every orb is a static frame. Nothing glows; the dots are the only ink.
